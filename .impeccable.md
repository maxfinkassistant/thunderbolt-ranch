# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users
Front Range Colorado families buying beef in bulk for the first time — plus the friend groups they recruit to split a cow. They arrive anxious about three things: cost, freezer space, and "what do I actually get." Browsed at home, usually daytime, often on a phone passed around a kitchen table.

### Brand Personality
Honest, weathered, appetizing. Thunderbolt Ranch talks like a rancher who has answered the same phone questions for twenty seasons: plainspoken, generous with trade-offs, zero marketing gloss. The butcher's ticket — monospaced, itemized, exact — is a core brand artifact.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm print-shop naturalism. Cream paper ground, deep umber ink, rust red as the working accent, brass and sage as supporting tones. Light theme only. Feels like a well-set feed-store broadsheet crossed with a modern checkout. Anti-references: generic DTC pastel farm brands, dark "premium steakhouse" sites, anything glassy.

Type: Young Serif for display (warm cookbook slab), Albert Sans for UI/body, Spline Sans Mono for ticket labels, numbers, and tags.

### Design Principles
1. Explain the trade before the sale — every choice shows what you gain and what you give up.
2. Numbers are the product — weights, dates, dollars set in mono, always visible, never hidden behind "contact us."
3. One decision per screen region; progressive disclosure over walls of options.
4. The cow diagram is the navigation — spatial understanding beats menu labels.
5. Group buying is social proof made literal — show the cow filling up.
