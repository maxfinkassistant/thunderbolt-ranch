  and patty poundage now print on an appended "SPECIAL REQUESTS" page instead.
- Grocery shelf prices in `GROCERY_CUTS` (`src/data/config.ts`) are survey estimates
  calibrated to the USDA Choice average already on the site. Replace with real observed
  Front Range prices before leaning on the savings number in marketing.
- Where a "most popular" option was called out, it is now also the pre-selected default
  (top round and bottom round now default to grind, sirloin tip to steaks, arm to grind).
  Say the word if you'd rather badge popularity without moving the default.
- **Ground beef is close to break-even against the shelf** at the assumed $8.49/lb for
  85/15 — it's the biggest line by weight, so the whole savings number swings on it. The
  table shows that row as "about even" rather than a fake win. Real shelf prices from a
  Greeley or Fort Collins store would firm this up fast.
- Savings at today's numbers: about $271 on a quarter, $542 on a half, $1,069 on a whole.
